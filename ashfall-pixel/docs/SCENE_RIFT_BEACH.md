# Scene brief: the rift and the beach

Pulled from Chapters V–IX (read in full), the Book 1 Greymark beat sheet (3.0), the Book 2 beat sheet, the Editor Notes, and a keyword search of the Valencourt beat sheet. Short quotes are from the text. Read on 4 Nov 2026.

## Decision needed first: which rift?

| | **The beach rift** [v2.45, Ch V–IX] | **The young rift** [3.0 beat sheet] |
|---|---|---|
| Way in | Under the boulder church: dungeons, poison lake, eyeball-domed chapel, a tear over the altar | Harren's cellar, a sealed stair, warm stone corridors, the cracked lid, a tear |
| Inside | Open: a long grey beach, endless twilight, stars, sea, an island, dunes, a forest | Enclosed: "Small, dark, flooded. Warm red shallows" |
| Who's there | Phantoms of the trio, body-spiders, Aldren and two guards, angel-Vex on a platform | Husks of the missing in rows, a malformed crystal being, Vex in a crystal cage |
| Climax | Duel in the rising tide, demon-Aldren, the thurible | Vex offers the thurible; he kills her; the song breaks |

**The beach exists only in v2.45.** In v2.45 the trio die on that beach ("*the* Rift"). In 3.0 they die at an offstage "Greystone", and the Greymark rift has no beach. The books never say whether Greystone *is* the beach.

**Suggestion:** build the beach as **Greystone, the memory**: where the trio died, seen in the dreams and in the "Wren's voice bleeds in" beat of Ch III. It's the most striking setting in the books. The young rift can be a second, smaller scene.

## Getting in (v2.45)
- **The chapel:** "A stone arch leaned aggressively over the entrance of a building half-sunk into the rock. Its round roof bulged up from the stone like a carved eyeball." Inside: "A pale colorless light", worshippers waist-deep in water.
- **The tear:** "A thin black crack hung in the air above the altar… Red water wept from it." It grows "twice the size of a man wide"; "black tendrils… the darkness distorting the light and space around itself", pulsing "as if alive".
- **The crossing:** "My body compressing into a point. Falling… through a lightless void." Then "a thin, high whine" and "water lapping against stone".
- **The arrival cave:** "hemispherical", "bright pale light that seemed to emanate from nowhere", a "small pulsing stream" of scarlet water.
- **The disc:** "a great disc of stone, wide as a threshing floor… black depths that dropped away on every side". It is carved in rings divided like cartwheel spokes, showing "Men bowing. Waves. Things with too many legs." At its centre: "an eye, carved wide open."

## The beach: layout (front to back)
1. **The cave mouth** (front-left): a few steps from the surf.
2. **The beach:** "A long grey beach curved away into the dusk". The sand is grey, soft and loose, with "knee high dune grass" behind. Marin's blood stains it "black".
3. **The sea** (along one side, to the horizon): "Small waves… broke against the shore with a tired hush." The tide comes in during the duel.
4. **Three graves:** "three humps in the sand"; Kethric's helm "lay amongst the sands, a token for a grave marker".
5. **The silk temple:** "a small temple covered in crystalline silk", "at the edge of the coast". Wren was dragged here.
6. **The platform and temple terrace** (up the coast): "jutting gently out into the sea", stone steps and stonework, an altar. "Scarlet blood pooled at the base of the altar" beside "a pile of bodies" (the mill workers in blue coats). From afar it reads as "a twinkle of white light"; close up it is royal blue and gold.
7. **The island** (offshore, on the horizon): "A distant black mass". When lit: "A bone-white scaffold outlined its greater form", with spiders that "layered butchered flesh onto the sleeping giant". This is the apostle of the Unborn One, a titan.
8. **The forest** (behind the dunes): "the dark forest that no matter how far you walked would always remain out of reach".
9. **The sky:** "Permanent twilight", "packed with stars… this wasn't the sky I knew", no clouds. The Drunk Goat constellation: "The crooked line, the familiar shape… It twinkled at me."

**No canon for:** cliffs, a moon, the colour of the forest or the sea, or the platform's exact size.

## Objects and light
- **Chalice:** blue light, held in the angel's right hand, "ever-flowing", pouring into the sea.
- **Thurible:** gold, smoking, on "a golden chain" in her left hand. It speaks "Burn / Cleanse / Purify".
- **The pair of them:** "Bathed in twin luminescence of royal blue and pure gold".
- **The sword on fire:** "Thick liquid fire" ("dark red fire" in 3.0).

## Sound and time
- **Sound:** a chant of "low voices" in his head, and the "tired hush" of the waves.
- **Time runs faster inside:** Kethric once spent years there.
- **Mood:** "accursed place", "bleak sands".

## Moments the scene could show (in story order)
1. He lands on the disc, steps out and drops to his knees in the sand (Ch VI).
2. The phantoms replay: Desso dies; Marin dies saving Wren in the dune grass; Wren is dragged to the silk temple (Ch VI).
3. At the empty graves he leaves his helm (Ch VII).
4. He walks up the coast to the beacon: Aldren, the guards, the corpse pile, the angel (Ch VII).
5. The angel lights up the island and reveals the titan (Ch VII).
6. The estoc duel in the rising tide under the Drunk Goat (Ch VIII).
7. The thurible sets him alight; Aldren transforms (Ch VIII).
8. The burning-sword fight (Ch IX).

## The young rift [3.0] (second scene)
- **Way down:** Harren's cellar, then "a sealed stair broken open", then stone "older and warmer" covered in carvings.
- **The deepest chamber:** "Red water pours from a black wound in the air, ragged edges, inky dark. The lid the ancients built lies cracked open beside it." Going through is "like pushing through jelly".
- **Inside:** "Small, dark, flooded. Warm red shallows. Husks of the missing sitting in rows, mouths open", eleven of Vex's workers among them. A malformed being: "crystal half-grown through a body". The song is at full volume.
- **Vex:** "At the heart, inside a cage of crystal, winged and many-eyed", her face pressed to the crystal. She holds the chalice "weeping red" and the thurible "smoking".
- **The ending:** the chalice cracks dry, and the rift spits him onto the cellar floor.
- **Planned (Valencourt sheet):** "the stars in the rift sky are souls… a vast shape behind the stars (ox skull, arms raised) begins to conduct".
